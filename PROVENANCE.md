# Provenance and evidentiary limits

- Figure means: transcribed from the attached original "Figure 4" through "Figure 8" workbook sheets, no interpolation or synthetic uncertainty. Figure 7 source labels are A/B/C/D, not validated questionnaire dimension names; Figure 8 source labels E/F/G, interpreted as time/engagement/retention only following the supplied manuscript narrative. Check the actual questionnaire/data dictionary before labeling revised figures.
- Qwen model: official repository `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` at the SHA in `configs/model.json` (see pinned official config https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct/blob/4709f6c0771f0185a675b046268cdc1d1f2c74ce/config.json).
- The article-revision draft describes candidate sample sizes, statistical outputs and supplementary experiments; these are NOT imported as verified measurements. No synthetic case-level research outcomes, p-values, ICCs, effect sizes, or teacher ratings are presented as observed data.
- The public repository contains pipelines and schemas. Private student-level data, ETS licensed responses, EFCAMDAT restricted records, and voice files must remain outside Git.
- Mixed-model and randomization outputs require original eight classroom assignments and student records; six-condition crossover inference requires actual within-person observations.
- The scripts produce their own outputs when you supply valid authorized data and do not reproduce the manuscript's numerical inferences without those inputs.
